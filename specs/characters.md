# Characters

## Player Character

- **Appearance**: A circle shape
- **Properties**:
  - Health points
  - Movement capability in all directions
  - Attack capability
  - Interaction capability
- **Role**: The player controls this character to fight enemies directly, build and sell towers, and interact with diplomats

## Enemy Types

Enemy units are categorized into three main types:

### 1. Small Units

- **Appearance**: Small circles
- **Properties**:
  - Low health (can be killed with one hit)
  - Moderate speed
  - Basic damage to player and towers
- **Behavior**:
  - Move toward player's camp or towers
  - Attack player and towers when in range
- **Notes**: Good source of early souls when killed by the player

### 2. Medium Units

- **Appearance**: Medium-sized circles
- **Properties**:
  - Medium health
  - Medium speed
  - Medium damage to player and towers
- **Behavior**:
  - Move toward player's camp or towers
  - Attack player and towers when in range

### 3. Guardian Units

- **Appearance**: Larger circles
- **Properties**:
  - High health
  - Lower speed
  - Higher damage to player and towers
- **Behavior**:
  - Protect camps and buildings
  - Attack player when in range

## Special Characters

### Diplomats

- **Appearance**: Circle with distinctive marking
- **Properties**:
  - Non-attackable
  - Stationary position within camps
- **Role**: Allow player to interact with camps for:
  - Accepting quests
  - Checking quest status
  - Establishing cooperation
  - Changing target camps for cooperating camps

### The King

- **Appearance**: Large, distinctive circle
- **Properties**:
  - Very high health
  - High damage output
  - Special abilities (to be defined)
- **Role**: Final boss of the game, located behind a barrier that opens when all camps are conquered
- **Behavior**:
  - Attacks player and friendly units
  - Defends his chamber
